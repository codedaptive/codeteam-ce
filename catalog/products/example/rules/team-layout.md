# Team-acme-widgets Layout

- **Repo:** `acme-widgets`, base branch `main`, checked out at `$CODETEAM_WORKSPACE/acme-widgets` (default
  `~/codeteam-work/acme-widgets`).
- **Tiers:** `exchange` and `integration` under `$CODETEAM_WORKSPACE/team-acme-widgets/`. Each lane works in its
  standing worktree under `workcrews/<lane>/`.
- **Your worktree is your write surface.** Never check out or branch inside it; the coordinator creates worktrees.
